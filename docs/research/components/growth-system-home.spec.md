# GrowthSystemHome Spec

Component: `src/components/site/GrowthSystemHome.tsx`

## Role

Primary Bartlett Labs landing page modeled after the RhinoBot conversion structure, with original Bartlett Labs positioning and no RhinoBot brand assets.

## Sections

- Hero: dark operating-room style layout with headline, CTAs, metrics, and CSS-built lead console.
- Ticker: repeated operational proof points.
- Problem: missed-call and follow-up pain points.
- System: three operating engines from `systemEngines`.
- Metrics: compact proof band.
- Industries: 12 linked industry cards.
- Demo proof: video/demo placeholders for Bartlett Labs assets.
- Founder: Kyle/Bartlett Labs credibility section with local positioning.
- How it works: 4-step implementation flow.
- Service areas: Houston-area directory.
- Pricing: 3 tiers from `pricingTiers`.
- Expansion modules: add-on growth engines.
- Security: data ownership and consent posture.
- Final CTA: booking and phone paths.

## Design Rules

- No rhino mascot or RhinoBot naming.
- Use dark hero, orange CTA, dense cards, and dropdown depth from the reference site.
- Keep cards flat and functional with radius 8px or less for new growth UI.
- Demo section exists because Bartlett Labs has proof assets and the reference site lacks that layer.
