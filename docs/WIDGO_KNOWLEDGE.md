## What RepBot does

RepBot is the AI voice receptionist. The business keeps its existing phone number and forwards it (always, or only when unanswered or after hours). RepBot answers 24/7 in English with a natural neural voice, answers common questions about services, hours, and service area, captures the caller's name, number, address, and problem on every call, and books the job straight into an open slot on the connected calendar while the caller is still on the line. When a caller does not book, RepBot logs them as a lead so the team can call back. Every call is saved with a transcript and a short AI summary.

Setup is done for the customer: Kyle calls within one business day to forward the line, connect the calendar, and load services, hours, and service area. No hardware, no IT. The owner can change the greeting, the voice, and how it books at any time by asking.

RepBot is built for local service businesses: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, garage doors, restoration, appliance repair, and similar trades, and it also fits dental and medical offices, salons, and law firms.

RepBot pricing: $149 per month with no setup fee. That includes 500 call minutes a month (about 200 calls); after that it is 20 cents a minute. Month to month, no contract.

Prospects can hear RepBot before buying. The demo line is (979) 987-4241, the same public Bartlett Labs number, and RepBot answers it. Offer that number or the RepBot site (https://repbot.bartlettlabs.io) when a visitor wants to hear it or asks what it sounds like.

## What the AI Growth System does

Missed-call text-back: when a call goes unanswered, the caller gets a text back within about 2 minutes that acknowledges them, asks what they need, and moves toward booking. The owner gets an alert. This runs around the clock and is included in every plan.

AI voice intake: RepBot answers when the owner can't, collects the job details, books on the calendar, and hands over a ready-to-call lead. Included in Crew and Herd. Available on Solo as a $149 per month add-on.

24/7 lead capture from phone calls, website chat, web forms, and text messages, routed into one pipeline.

Automated quote and estimate follow-up: text and email sequences that keep following up on open estimates until the customer books or says no (Crew and Herd).

Scheduling and booking on a connected calendar with confirmations and reminders.

Google review requests sent automatically after completed jobs.

Owner alerts and a simple dashboard showing new leads, missed calls, follow-up status, and booked jobs. Solo includes a weekly owner summary; Crew and Herd include the live pipeline dashboard with alerts.
