# Master Video Script — Bartlett Labs Outreach
### Record once. Swap screen share per business. Under 45 seconds total.

---

## How It Works

You record **3 short segments** on your phone. They get stitched together with a screen recording of each prospect's landing page in the middle. The screen share is the only part that changes per business.

```
[INTRO on camera] → [TRANSITION on camera] → [SCREEN SHARE of their landing page] → [CLOSE on camera]
     8 sec                  7 sec                    15-20 sec                         10 sec
```

Total: ~40-45 seconds per video.

---

## Setup

- **Location**: Anywhere clean — your office, garage workshop, front porch. Natural light preferred. Avoid busy backgrounds.
- **Framing**: Chest-up, centered. Phone vertical (most prospects watch on their phone).
- **Audio**: Quiet room. No music. Your natural voice.
- **Wardrobe**: Whatever you'd wear to meet a client. Polo or button-down, no hoodie.
- **Eye line**: Look at the camera lens, not the screen.
- **Energy**: Relaxed, conversational. Like you're talking to your neighbor across the fence. NOT a sales pitch.

---

## Segment 1: INTRO (8 seconds)

> **"Hey — I'm Kyle. I run Bartlett Labs here in Crosby, Texas."**

That's it. Smile. Casual nod. No rush.

**Tips:**
- Small pause after "Hey" — makes it feel natural, not rehearsed
- Say "Crosby, Texas" clearly — locality is your superpower
- Record 3-4 takes, pick the most natural one

---

## Segment 2: TRANSITION (7 seconds)

> **"I spend a lot of time looking at local businesses online, and I put something together for yours."**

**Tips:**
- Slight lean forward on "put something together" — shows genuine interest
- This bridges into the screen share, so end with a natural pause
- Don't say "website" or "mockup" — keep it vague to build curiosity

---

## Segment 3: SCREEN SHARE (15-20 seconds)

**You do NOT record this segment on camera.** This is a screen recording of their specific landing page.

For each prospect, screen-record yourself:
1. Opening their personalized landing page
2. Slowly scrolling through it (2-3 seconds per section)
3. Pausing briefly on the hero section and any before/after content

**Optional voiceover** (record separately or skip entirely — the visual sells itself):
> "Here's what I put together — custom design, mobile-friendly, built to actually get you calls."

**Automation option**: This can be automated later with Puppeteer/Playwright scrolling through each landing page and recording. For batch 1, just do it manually — takes 30 seconds per prospect.

---

## Segment 4: CLOSE (10 seconds)

> **"I'm not trying to sell you anything — I just thought you'd want to see it. If it's interesting, there's a link below to grab 15 minutes with me."**

**Tips:**
- "I'm not trying to sell you anything" — say this like you mean it. It's the most important line.
- Slight shrug on "if it's interesting" — casual, no pressure
- "Link below" — they'll see the calendar link in the email
- End with a natural smile and small nod. No "thanks" or "have a great day" — just stop. Clean ending.

---

## Recording Checklist

- [ ] Find quiet spot with good natural light
- [ ] Phone vertical, chest-up framing
- [ ] Record Segment 1 (INTRO) — 3-4 takes, pick best
- [ ] Record Segment 2 (TRANSITION) — 3-4 takes, pick best
- [ ] Record Segment 4 (CLOSE) — 3-4 takes, pick best
- [ ] Screen record one landing page as a test (Segment 3)
- [ ] Watch the full stitched version — does it feel like a neighbor, not a salesman?

## Industry-Specific Transition Variants (Optional)

If you want to record a few extra transition lines to swap in for specific industries, here are options. These replace Segment 2:

**For restaurants/BBQ:**
> "I've been checking out restaurants around Crosby, and I put something together for yours."

**For contractors/trades (plumbing, HVAC, roofing, tile):**
> "I keep running into contractors around here who do great work but are invisible online. So I put something together for you."

**For auto/detailing:**
> "I was looking at auto shops in the area, and yours stood out — so I put something together."

**For pest control/lawn care:**
> "I noticed a lot of service companies around here are hard to find online. So I put something together for yours."

These are optional. The generic Segment 2 works for everyone. But if you have 10 extra minutes, recording 3-4 of these variants makes each video feel even more personal.

---

## Stitching the Final Video

**Manual method (batch 1):**
1. Record segments 1, 2, 4 on your phone
2. Screen record each prospect's landing page
3. Use CapCut (free), iMovie, or any editor to stitch: Intro → Transition → Screen Share → Close
4. Export and attach to email or host on a simple page

**Automated method (batch 2+):**
1. Use the master intro/transition/close clips
2. Automate screen recording with Puppeteer/Playwright
3. Use ffmpeg to concatenate clips per prospect
4. Bulk generate videos with a script

We can build the automation after batch 1 proves the concept.
