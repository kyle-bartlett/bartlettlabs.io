*QUESTION 1: Is a personalized landing page + Loom video enough for initial outreach?*

Short answer: *Yes — but with additions, it becomes a killer combo.*

The research is clear: personalized video outreach gets 25-30% reply rates vs 1-5% for text-only cold email. That's a 6x difference. Personalized landing pages drive up to 202% higher conversions vs generic pages. So your instinct is dead-on — this is exactly the right play.

But here's what separates "good" from "great":

*What you NEED (your current plan — solid):*
• Personalized Loom-style video mentioning their business name + specific issues
• Custom landing page showing what you'd build for them
• AI video clone to scale this without recording 100+ individual videos

*What you should ADD:*
• A *free mini-audit* of their current website (speed, mobile, SEO, Google reviews) — this is the "Trojan Horse" that gets them to watch
• *One specific problem* you spotted on their site (broken link, slow load, no mobile menu, no Google listing) — proof you actually looked
• A *clear CTA* — not "let me know what you think" but "book your free 10-min audit here: [link]"
• A *follow-up sequence* (3-5 touches over 2 weeks) — 80% of deals happen after the 5th touchpoint

*The data says:*
• Cold emails with personalized video get 3-5x higher reply rates
• Smaller, targeted campaigns (under 50 recipients) get 5.8% response vs 2.1% for 1000+ blasts
• It takes 8-12 touchpoints to book a meeting with a cold prospect
• Agencies sending 15-20 personalized messages daily land their first client within 30-45 days

*QUESTION 2: What other steps would I take if I were you?*

Here's what I'd do differently/additionally:

*1. Lead with the audit, not the pitch*
Don't say "I build websites." Say "I found 3 things costing you customers — here's a free video showing what they are." The audit IS your pitch. Nobody wants to be sold to. Everyone wants free help.

*2. Add a "before/after" mock-up*
For your top 10 prospects, spend 15 minutes creating a quick mockup of what their new site COULD look like. Even a Figma screenshot or a quick component from your own site adapted with their colors/logo. This is the "wow" factor that closes deals.

*3. Use Google Reviews as your opener*
You already enriched your GHL contacts with Google review data. Use it: "Hey [Name], I noticed [Business] has a 4.8 on Google with 47 reviews — that's awesome. But your website doesn't show any of them. That's leaving money on the table..."

*4. Build social proof FAST*
Do 1-2 projects at cost or free for local businesses you actually care about. Get video testimonials. These become your outreach ammunition.

*5. Create a "results" page on bartlettlabs.io*
Before/after screenshots, speed improvements, booking increases. Real numbers. Even estimates work: "We estimate this redesign could capture 30% more mobile visitors."

*6. Warm up before the cold outreach*
Follow their social accounts, leave genuine comments on their posts, share their content for 1-2 weeks before you send the cold email. When your name shows up in their inbox, it won't be completely cold.

*7. Ask for referrals EVERY time*
Even from people who say no. "No worries at all — do you know any other business owners who might be looking to update their online presence?" Referrals close at 30%+ vs 1-2% for cold.

*QUESTION 3: Outreach Strategy Plan*

*Phase 1: Prep (This Weekend)*
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

• Record ONE high-quality Loom intro video (45-60 seconds max)
  - Introduce yourself naturally (name, Bartlett Labs, Crosby TX)
  - Explain what you do in one sentence: "I help local businesses get more customers through better websites and smart automation"
  - Show 1-2 quick examples of your work
  - End with: "I put together something specific for your business — check it out below"

• Set up your AI video clone tool
  - *Recommended: HeyGen* ($29/mo) — best bang for buck, affordable for bootstrapping
  - *Upgrade path: Tavus* ($97+/mo) — better quality, uses your real face, but pricier
  - Train the clone on your intro video

• Build your personalized landing page template
  - Header: "[Business Name] — Your New Online Presence"
  - Side-by-side: their current site screenshot vs your mockup
  - 3 bullet points of what you'd improve
  - Social proof section (your portfolio, reviews)
  - CTA: "Book Your Free Audit" → links to your calendar

*ORGANIC CHANNELS (Free — Time Investment Only)*
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

*LinkedIn (Highest ROI for B2B — your #1 channel)*
• Post 3-5x per week from your PERSONAL profile (not company page — personal gets 8x more engagement)
• Content types that work:
  - "Before/after" website transformations (screenshot posts)
  - "I just helped [business type] do [result]" stories
  - Hot takes on local business tech ("Most Crosby businesses don't even have a mobile-friendly website. Here's why that's costing them...")
  - Behind-the-scenes of building (people love watching the process)
  - Quick tip threads ("3 things every small business website needs in 2026")
• Engage 15-20 min/day: comment on local business owners' posts, industry posts, tech posts
• Connect with 10-20 relevant people daily (business owners, local entrepreneurs, marketers)
• Personal profiles generate 8x more engagement than company pages
• Employee content gets 2x more click-throughs than company page posts

*Instagram/Facebook (Local Visibility)*
• Share the same content adapted for visual format
• Reels/short videos of website builds, quick tips, before/afters
• Join and engage in local business Facebook groups (Crosby, Baytown, Houston East)
• Post helpful advice in groups — don't pitch, just help. People will check your profile.

*Google Business Profile*
• Set up Bartlett Labs on Google Maps if you haven't
• Post weekly updates (Google Posts)
• Ask happy clients for Google reviews
• This is CRITICAL for local search — "web developer near me" searches

*Content Marketing (Long-term SEO play)*
• Blog on bartlettlabs.io — 1-2 posts per month
• Target keywords local businesses search: "do I need a website for my [type] business", "website cost for small business", "how to get more customers online"
• Each blog post becomes 3-5 social media posts

*Community & Networking*
• Crosby/Huffman Chamber of Commerce (if one exists)
• Local BNI or networking groups
• Attend local events, farmers markets, small business meetups
• Offer to do a free "Digital Presence Workshop" at a local library or co-working space

*PAID CHANNELS (When Budget Allows)*
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

*Facebook/Instagram Ads ($5-10/day when ready)*
• Target: Business owners in Crosby, Baytown, Huffman, Humble, Kingwood (25-mile radius)
• Ad type: Video ad showing a before/after website transformation
• CTA: "Get Your Free Website Audit"
• Retarget: Anyone who visited bartlettlabs.io but didn't book

*Google Ads ($10-15/day when ready)*
• Target keywords: "website design crosby tx", "web developer near me", "small business website houston"

*90-DAY ROADMAP*
