- Portfolio section: Gallery of 14 landing pages you've built

- Custom demo of their specific business (you've already built these for prospects)
- Sent as follow-up after call → "Here's how it would work specifically for you"
- Outcome: Convert call → contract

## What To Record (Eliminate Duplicate Work)

### YouTube Videos (Weeks 1-2)
**Format:** 80% talking head (you), 20% GHL demo  
**Tools:** Phone camera (you talking) + screen share (GHL system as proof)  
**Content:** Videos 1-5 from your script (already written)  
**Time to film:** ~2 hours total (30 min per video with no editing)  
**Editing:** Minimal — cut out pauses, add simple title cards  
**What to show on GHL screen:**
- Missed call → instant text notification
- Chatbot responding
- Lead moving through pipeline

### Website Demo Videos
**Format:** 30-60 second clips from YouTube  
**How to create:** Screen record YouTube playback, clip to 30-60s, upload to website  
**Placement:** Homepage hero + portfolio pages  
**What you're showing:** The "best part" of each YouTube video (the mechanism working)  
**Time to create:** ~1 hour total (just clipping, no new recording)

### Landing Pages
**Format:** Already exist (Pickens, Santiago's, 5 Stars, etc.)  
**What you do:** Create a gallery on your website  
- Screenshot or embed the live site
- "Built for: Pickens Premier Pressure Washing"
- "Built for: Santiago's Auto Repair"
- Link to the actual site if it's live
**Time to create:** ~30 min (screenshot + gallery HTML)

## Key Principles (Don't Skip These)

1. **One source of truth for video:** YouTube videos are the master copy. Website clips come FROM YouTube, not in addition to it.
2. **You're local:** That's your edge. Every email emphasizes Crosby, founder-led, you show up.
3. **Portfolio is proof:** Your 14 landing pages are your portfolio. Showcase them visually on your website.
4. **Fast follow-up matters:** Anyone who comments "BOOKED" gets a DM in <1 hour. Anyone who replies to cold email gets a call offer in <4 hours.
5. **Measure everything:** Track opens, clicks, calls, conversions. Refine based on what works.
