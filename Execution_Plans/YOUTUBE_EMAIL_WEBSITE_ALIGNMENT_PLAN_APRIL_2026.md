# Bartlett Labs Launch Alignment Plan
Date: April 24, 2026

## Core Decision

Lead with one simple wedge:

**Missed calls equal missed sales. Bartlett Labs installs a simple missed-call recovery and fast-follow-up system for local service businesses.**

Keep the YouTube strategy. Align the emails, website, Loom videos, and demos around the same idea for the first 30 days.

The landing pages still matter. Use them as personalized proof for the top 50 prospects. In the opening email, the main point stays missed-call recovery. The landing page becomes the extra evidence that you already looked at their business and can build useful assets for them.

## Difference Between YouTube Videos and Website Demos

YouTube videos create belief.

- They teach the pain
- They explain the mechanism
- They make the owner think, "This is happening in my business"
- They bring cold people into your world

Website demos create proof.

- They show the thing working
- They help a prospect trust the email
- They support the booking decision
- They should be shorter and more direct

Use clips from YouTube as secondary content. Record the homepage demo separately so it feels crisp and purpose-built.
