**The Goal:** Confidently launch the "5-Minute Booking Engine" (Missed Call Text-Back) to local service businesses in the Crosby area without confusing prospects with enterprise AI jargon or advanced workflow consulting.

**The Positioning:** 
*   **Who you are:** Kyle Bartlett, a Crosby-based founder and systems builder who cut his teeth in Fortune 500 operations (Sears, Belk, Apple, Anker) and now builds practical systems for local trades.
*   **What you sell:** A straightforward, automated system that stops leads from slipping through the cracks.

## 2. The Core Offer
**The 5-Minute Booking Engine** (Powered by Go High Level bots)
*   **Pricing:** ~$1,500-$5,000 setup fee + $99-$200/month (Accessible entry point to get a foot in the door).
*   **Component 1: Missed Call Text-Back:** "If you didn't answer, you didn't get the job." Instantly texts back missed calls to keep the lead warm while the owner is on a job site.
*   **Component 2: Automated Follow-Ups:** Automated text sequences check in on open quotes so the owner doesn't have to play phone tag.
*   **Component 3: Simple CRM Dashboard:** A clean mobile/web dashboard to see every lead, quote, and booked job in one place.
*   **The Trojan Horse:** The high-end Next.js website concepts you've built (Santiago's Auto, 5 Stars Electric, Doggie World) are now positioned as *wrappers* or *add-ons* to the Booking Engine, providing a professional front door that feeds directly into the automated system.
