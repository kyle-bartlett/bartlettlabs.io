Our Story

La Cruda Brand BBQ started with a simple belief: the best BBQ comes from patience, quality ingredients, and recipes passed down through generations.

Born in Crosby, Texas, we combine traditional Texas pit-smoking techniques with bold Mexican-American flavors that make our BBQ unlike anything else in Harris County.

Every cut is hand-selected, every rub is mixed in-house, and every brisket gets the full 14 hours it deserves over post oak wood.
