 The Core Sequence
    
    Email 1: The Initial Hook (Day 1)
    (This one swaps out based on their industry—HVAC, plumbing, restaurants, etc. Here is the HVAC variant as an example)
    > Subject: Gary, how Crosby Air can book more jobs automatically
    >
    > Hi Gary,
    > 
    > I'm Kyle with Bartlett Labs — I build automations for HVAC businesses here in Crosby.
    > 
    > I know running an HVAC business means you're on the job all day and can't always answer the phone. That's where a lot of leads get lost. Here's what I set up for businesses like yours:
    > 
    > - Missed call text-back — when you can't pick up, the customer instantly gets a text saying "Hey, we got your call — how can we help?" Keeps the lead warm
    > - Online booking — customers pick a time slot right from your website or Google listing
    > - Automated follow-ups — after the job, customers get a review request. After a quote, they get a gentle "still interested?" nudge
    > 
    > One-time build. You own everything. No monthly platform fees. Most setups run $800-$1,500.
    > 
    > Got 10 minutes for a call? Happy to swing by.
    > 
    > Kyle Bartlett  
    > Bartlett Labs | bartlettlabs.io
