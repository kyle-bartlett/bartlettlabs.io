## Phone Call Script (30-60 seconds)

### Opening

> "Hi, is this **{{owner_first}}**? Hey {{owner_first}}, this is Kyle from Bartlett Labs — I'm a local software developer here in {{city}}. I'm not selling ads or marketing, I actually build custom automations for {{service_type}} businesses. Got 60 seconds?"

### If they say yes:

> "So here's the deal — I know you're busy running {{company_name}}, and a lot of times the phone rings while you're on a job and that lead just calls the next guy. What I build is a system that automatically texts that customer back saying 'Hey, we got your call, how can we help?' — so you never lose a lead.
>
> I also set up things like automatic Google review requests after every job, online booking, and a simple dashboard to track all your customers. One-time build, you own it, no monthly fees.
>
> Would you be open to a quick 10-minute meeting this week? I can come to your shop."

### If they say no / busy:

> "Totally understand. Can I text you a quick summary? That way you can check it out whenever you have a minute."

### Objection: "How much?"

> "Most setups run $500-1,500 depending on what you need. No monthly fees — you own everything. I can show you exactly what you'd get in a quick meeting."

### Objection: "I don't need a website"

> "That's fine — a lot of what I do isn't websites. It's behind-the-scenes automation. Like when a customer calls and you miss it, they automatically get a text back. Or after a job, they get a review request. Stuff that saves you time and gets you more 5-star reviews without you doing anything extra."

### Objection: "Send me an email"

> "Absolutely. What's the best email for you?"
> *(This is the gold — we get their actual email for follow-up)*
